# EARN - project status report, 23 September 2026

Supersedes the 20 September report. Repository: github.com/ananyac9820/fedrare (branch
`earn/complete`) · site: fedrare.vercel.app (updates on the next deploy of this branch).
Every figure is computed from the repository's own result files (`docs/results/`); nothing is
estimated. Every change to the design doc's plan was logged in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md` before the run
it affects.

## 1. Summary

The critical path from the 20 Sep report is closed, and Weeks 2-5 of the plan have run:

- **G0a failed after its one retry** (bias row: -0.463; a sign-aware amendment: 0.110). Per the
  design doc, the project moves to **Fallback F1**.
- **G0b passed on its retry**: fine-tuning DenseNet-121's last dense block by FedAvg lifts the
  Tier A baseline from 0.415 to **0.535** balanced accuracy.
- **G1 failed on both splits** - on S1 the attack gains 5x weight but barely moves rare F1; on S2 it
  cuts rare F1 by 0.21-0.24 but also balanced accuracy by 0.051, too visible for the 0.03 bar. The
  pre-registered framing is therefore Fallback F1, consistent with G0a.
- The **F1 study** ran in full: 8 methods x 4 attacks x S1/S2 x 3 seeds (192 runs), with four
  measured findings (README).
- **EARN is built** (`src/federated/earn.py`, unit-tested per step) and, by team decision (D5), was
  run on an **oracle** evidence signal with ablations and sensitivity (216 runs). It does not meet
  the G2 conditions even so; the mechanisms are understood (paper draft 7.5). G2 itself is not
  evaluable.
- **Smart contract** `EarnLedger` on local Hardhat: 5 contract tests; 2,400 real EARN rounds
  replayed, ~184k gas and ~1.1 ms per round; forged boosts rejected in every run.
- Paper draft, README, figures and website updated.

## 6. How to reproduce

See README "Reproduce". Whole pipeline on an Apple-silicon laptop: ~5 min feature extraction,
~13 min G0b retry, ~10 min for the 408-run grid, ~1 min chain replay.

## 7. Limitations to keep stating

EARN is not validated. All results are Tier A. S2 is constructed. The rare-class rule was chosen
after seeing counts. The 100-round schedule and the sign-aware variant are amendments.
